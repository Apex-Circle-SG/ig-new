# Payment

## Payment Stacks

|Roles|Providers|Examples|Function|
|---|---|---|---|
|Terminal Manager||- Verifone<br>- NMI<br>- Ingenico<br>- Worldline<br>- Aevi<br>- RMX|Manages, updates, and monitors POS terminals\.|
|Routing and Switching||- ACI Worldwide<br>- Vocalink \(Mastercard\)<br>- Euronet|Connectivity and routing layer in the payment transaction infrastructure\. Routes payment transaction messages\.|
|Payment Service Provider \(PSP\)|- PayPal<br>- Ayden<br>- Stripe<br>- Helcim<br>- Square<br>- Toast|- PayPal<br>- Adyen<br>- Stripe<br>- Square<br>- Braintree<br>- Checkout\.com<br>- Mollie<br>- Tyro<br>- Payone<br>- PayU<br>- SumUp<br>- Nuvei|Provides merchants with integration to multiple payment methods, risk management, and reporting\.|
|PayFac|- PayPal<br>- Stripe<br>- Square<br>- Toast|- Fawry<br>- BlueSnap<br>- Razorpay<br>- Fortis<br>- WePay \(J\.P\. Morgan\)|Operates a master merchant account, onboards sub\-merchants, and manages risk and compliance on their behalf\.|
|Payment Gateway|- PayPal<br>- Ayden<br>- Stripe<br>- Helcim<br>- Square<br>- Toast|- CyberSource \(Visa\)<br>- Authorize\.net \(Visa\)<br>- Braintree \(PayPal\)<br>- Payoneer<br>- Mastercard Payment Gateway Services<br>- Shopify|Technical interface between merchant and acquirer, encrypts and routes transaction data\.|
|Orchestrator|- PayPal<br>- Ayden<br>- Stripe<br>|- Akurateco<br>- IXOPAY<br>- Tribe<br>- Primer<br>- Tranzzo<br>- Juspay<br>- Paynt<br>- Spreedly<br>- IFPOS|Central layer that manages routing across multiple PSPs and acquirers, optimizing transactions\.|
|Independent Software Vendor \(ISV\)|- PayPal<br>- Stripe<br>- Square<br>- Toast|- Zettle \(PayPal\)<br>- NCR<br>- Retail Pro<br>- Shopify<br>- Vend|Develops and sells software solutions that integrate with payment systems or POS hardware\.|
|Processor|- PayPala<br>- Ayden<br>- Stripe<br>- Helcim<br>- Square|- Fiserv<br>- Worldline<br>- Tietoevry<br>- Payoneer<br>- Nexi<br>- BPC<br>- Compass Plus<br>- TSYS<br>- RS2|Handles the technical processing of payment transactions between merchants, acquirers, and card networks, including authorization, clearing, and settlement\.|
|Fraud Solutions|- PayPal<br>- Ayden<br>- Stripe<br>- Helcim<br>- Square<br>- Toast|- Ravelin<br>- FICO<br>- Featurespace<br>- Feedzai<br>- Riskified<br>- Signifyd|Delivers tools to detect, prevent, and manage fraudulent transactions\.|
|Chargeback||- Chargebacks911<br>- Verifi \(Visa\)<br>- Chargeflow<br>- Ethoca \(Mastercard\)<br>- Justt<br>- Chargeback Gurus|Manages disputed transactions and chargeback processes on behalf of merchants\. Tracks, responds, and provides evidence to contest or resolve disputes\.|
|Other Services \(Billing, Dashboard, Business Intelligence\)||- Chargebee<br>- Zuora<br>- ChartMogul|Value\-added tools for operations, reporting, and analytics\.|
