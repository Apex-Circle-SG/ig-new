# Advertising and SEO release — 13 September 2026

Live at **https://insightginie.com**, supervised by `insightginie-web` on
**127.0.0.1:3000**. The stack is Next.js/TypeScript on Node.js.

## What shipped

- Advertising eligibility on **all 13 reviewed public pages**, including the
  homepage, calculator, data explorer and trust pages. Private tool documents,
  embeds, APIs, unfinished author pages and errors are excluded.
- Indexable server-rendered pages, canonical URLs, descriptive headings,
  structured data, internal links and a 13-URL sitemap. Preview deployments stay
  non-indexable. No thin programmatic city/occupation pages were generated.
- A [US income distribution explorer](https://insightginie.com/data/us-income-distribution/)
  with 44 source-derived bands, eight overview groups, sorting, CSV download and
  a script-free, attributed chart embed. It uses 2024 individual income from the
  2025 CPS ASEC, including US people age 15 and over with no income.
- Financial inputs and results inside an opaque sandbox. Advertising scripts
  cannot read that document. Calculations stay in the browser; only fixed event
  names, bounded dimensions and generic sharing cross the boundary.
- A server-calculated homepage example; the private calculator loads after
  **Try your income**. The full calculator remains immediately available.
- First-party aggregate event counters with no user IDs, financial values or
  analytics cookies. Files are private, retain 30 UTC dates, and have a separate
  daily cleanup timer. No Google Analytics integration is claimed.

Advertising starts by default in known regions where the configured policy
permits it. Required/unknown regions wait for a choice; withdrawal is respected.
**100% page coverage does not mean guaranteed impressions for every visitor.**
See [advertising implementation and account settings](advertising.md).

| Homepage | 91 | 100 | 100 | 100 | 2.83 s | 0 |
| Income calculator | 78 | 100 | 100 | 100 | 3.01 s | 0.014 |

[Lab report](verification/launch-release-lighthouse-summary.json). Earlier
measurements are preserved rather than replacing them with the most favorable
run. These local simulated-mobile measurements did not include filled Google
ads. The target of LCP below 2.5 seconds is not yet demonstrated, and field p75
Core Web Vitals have not been measured. Calculator JavaScript remains the main
performance follow-up.

Google Auto ads also introduced substantial reserved space in the live mobile
check. Account-side placement previews should be used to limit intrusive formats
and excess whitespace. The supplied publisher ID does not provide account
access, so approval status, Auto ads configuration and regional privacy-message
settings have not been audited. Actual ad rendering was observed independently.
