## Recommended reader structure

Keep the seven existing Business & Management entrances. The upload should enrich the existing subject collections, with two useful additions to discovery:

1. A **Build and review a business plan** reading index in **Strategy & Growth**. Its sequence should be: define the customer/problem; test an offer; choose the business model; plan delivery and people; review economics and funding; assess risks and ethics. This is a connected checklist linking the authoritative detailed pages, not a second copy of every framework. Group the relevant planning/venture readings together within Strategy & Growth; a separate venture subcollection can wait until its actual guide volume warrants it.

This material has enough distinct reader intent for its own entrance: ethical lenses; shareholder/stakeholder purpose; stakeholder influence and responsibility; CSR commitments and conduct; making and escalating difficult decisions. Keep links to existing stakeholder, culture, employee-experience, marketing and sustainability-reporting readings.

Do not create four reader-facing pages named after the documents. Do not add an eighth Business gateway, move these notes into Property, or classify all the mixed checklist as project management.

## Corrections, uncertainty and verification needed

- **Mintzberg:** the professional-bureaucracy row repeats “standardisation of skills” in the column for organizational part. The original research associates this configuration with skilled professionals in the operating core; distinguish mechanism from component. [Mintzberg’s original paper](https://doi.org/10.1287/mnsc.26.3.322).
- **Stakeholder attribution:** “Evan and Friedman” needs correction/checking against Evan and Freeman. The author bibliography documents work with William Evan; verify the exact 1988 quotation before presenting it as a direct quotation. [Freeman’s Darden bibliography](https://www.darden.virginia.edu/sites/default/files/inline-files/Freeman%2C%20Ed_CV2024.pdf).
- **Friedman:** check the 1962 attribution against the cited wording and the famous 1970 essay. Do not paraphrase the position as permission to ignore ethics or law. [Friedman archive](https://miltonfriedman.org/topics/business-and-profit).
- **Ethics comparisons:** the alleged higher returns, lower risk and innovation advantages/disadvantages are assertions in a study table, not supported universal outcomes. Shareholder and stakeholder approaches are not simply “profit versus responsibility.” The power/interest matrix should not imply that low-power affected people have no moral standing.
- **Company examples:** allegations concerning named companies need dated case-specific sources and outcomes. Retain the examples for review, without publishing unqualified or current blanket accusations. Corporate IP ownership and employment/discrimination examples also require jurisdiction and contract context.
- **TI decision prompts:** preserve the supplied historical attribution, but do not call the short checklist TI’s complete current code. The official current document covers a broader set of principles. [Texas Instruments code](https://www.ti.com/lit/ml/szzb178/szzb178.pdf).
- **Financial wording:** “earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation and amortisation (EBITA)” is a label mismatch; the listed expansion corresponds to EBITDA. “Retrain risk” appears intended to mean retain risk, but preserve the original and identify the editorial reading. The investor S$50k/S$25k versus S$100k/S$250k example is ambiguous and supplies no defined denominator or period.
- **Quantitative claims:** the “95% story / 5% deal” split has no supporting evidence. PoC/prototype/MVP time, revenue, budget and feedback statements are examples, not fixed definitions or guaranteed outcomes. The label “recession-proof” is a claim, not a demonstrated property.
- **Throughput relationship:** the source’s flow-time = WIP / throughput-rate expression needs consistent average quantities, units and flow-system conditions. Capacity and achieved throughput are not automatically identical.
- **Naming collisions:** this source expands DISC as Define/Identify/Segmentise/Classify. Do not merge it with DISC personality styles. “Solution–Product Fit,” COVENANT, SET-to-Grow and other labels need attribution and scope rather than an invented standard status.
- **Incomplete entries:** “replace” is explicitly a placeholder; “legality, legal counsel & paralegal” has no developed section; the organization-system and buffer headings rely on images; some strategic names have no explanation. Preserve these limits. Do not fill gaps with material claimed to have come from the upload.
