## Evidence and gaps

The source does not provide finished metaphysical essays, clinical trials, specimen records, complete archaeological studies or laboratory results. Context is labelled editorial, and prompts remain identifiable as supplied prompts.

- Freemasonry: date, editor and ritual edition are unknown; Centenary Lodge and English-authority references do not authenticate the version. The Royal Arch is mentioned but not supplied. Narrative penalties and the Hiram story are read as ritual material.
- The uploaded Kabbalah diagram’s creator and arrow annotations are unresolved. Its label/path placement is retained without declaring it universal. The linked Kabbalah Society page is context, not falsely credited as its origin.
- Gaza, Rockafella, Cicada 313, Polymeric Falcighol Derivation, Riffle, Nemesis, Singularity and Cognitive Infiltration are qualified or retained as unresolved where needed. Two website entries are unavailable placeholders and no external document relationships recover their links.
- Metaphysical, religious and alchemical ideas are distinguished from chemistry/nuclear mechanisms. No drug, radiation or pressure experiment is supplied or recommended.
- The Egg is linked to Andy Weir’s original fiction as a possible intended reference, not represented as an established scientific theory or a confirmed source of the prompt.
- Antisemitic motifs and the fabricated Protocols are identified explicitly; family names, religion and affiliations are not treated as evidence of collective wrongdoing.
- MKUltra’s documented Senate hearing is distinguished from unsubstantiated extensions. Agenda 21 is linked to the UN’s own conference account rather than treating a public document as proof of depopulation claims.
- The health charts are not medical guidance. They have foreground context identifying the false 5G/COVID link, MMS warnings and the need to assess individual interventions. Every medical allegation in their fine print has not been independently fact-checked.
- Historic ownership/political labels in the 2005 Bureau d’Études map are not current organizational facts. The uploaded chart editions and current creator editions are kept distinct.
