## Corrections and uncertainties

The reviewed explanations distinguish GDP/transactions/credit, quantity equations versus interest-rate relationships, both meanings of PPP, Kuznets cycles versus inequality, and discounted cash flow versus time-weighted returns. Named cycle periods, calendar anomalies and the Super Bowl association are not offered as trading signals. Unidentified proverbs, the philosopher taxonomy and the metaphysical “laws” retain their uncertainty rather than acquiring invented provenance.

Singapore reference checks identify material changes or errors including:

- Land Betterment Charge replacing DC/DP/TDL from August 2022.
- Residential property-tax schedules, BSD/ABSD and acquisition-date-specific SSD treatment; FTA eligibility is not a universal nationality exemption.
- CPF lease/age conditions, historical retirement sums, current HDB loan LTV and TDSR context, and SIBOR cessation.
- Standard versus Plus/Prime occupation/rental rules; the larger-property occupancy relaxation is extended through **31 December 2028**.
- EHG changes, the enhanced Silver Housing Bonus and the difference between lease buyback and right-sizing. Conflicting income references on official explanatory pages are disclosed rather than guessed.
- CEA’s 2026 16-hour CPD framework, DRS eligibility confusion, dual collective-sale consent measures, mortgage-duty terminology and the malformed rental GST expression.
- IRAS rental deductions from YA 2022, including qualifying first-tenant acquisition costs, replacing the blanket exclusion in the notes.

**Still uncertain or case-specific:** detailed planning/ancillary-use dimensions and fees; old notification periods; private contract interest/payment/compensation clauses; committee/quorum/repeat-sale procedure details; historic HDB quotas, grant/levy and scheme tables; professional fees, entry requirements and penalty figures; industrial scheme/approval thresholds; specific trust/covenant/tenancy assertions; and unidentified named laws or incomplete historical prompts. Each relevant page preserves the original detail and explains the limitation. No calculators use unverified old figures. This is an educational library, not a completed professional determination for an individual transaction.
