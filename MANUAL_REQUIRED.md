Updated 2026-09-13. The application now includes source-grounded Ask, six
deterministic finance tools, trust pages, consent-controlled aggregate analytics,
and a read-only WordPress preservation pipeline. All **9,071 inventoried public
posts and 17 pages** have local checksummed copies; **9,070 article candidates**
are normalized outside the application. The live migration pilot remains at
**2 noindex previews**, with their original blog canonicals. There has been
**no WordPress cutover**. See the [preservation handover](docs/audits/2026-09-13-migration-preservation/handover.md)
and [release acceptance](docs/ginie-migration-acceptance.md).
